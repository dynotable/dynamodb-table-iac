AWS CDK TableV2 (aws-cdk-lib 2.271) accepts a strongly consistent global table only when every region is in one of its region sets — US (us-east-1, us-east-2, us-west-2), EU (eu-west-1, eu-west-2, eu-west-3, eu-central-1), AP (ap-northeast-1, ap-northeast-2, ap-northeast-3); this table spans us-east-1, eu-west-1 and ap-northeast-1. Export it as Terraform or CloudFormation instead.
